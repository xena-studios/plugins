# The family test: xSample and xTwin on one server, each with its own relocated core, after
# xSample's own checks (scripts/boot-test.sh or scripts/boot-fabric.sh, two --checks files).
# xTwin recorded a made-up offline player in its player cache as it started.
logged: Recorded FamilyProbe in the family's player cache
# The family's files are where both look, and the platform's registry holds one board.
run: xtwin family
expect: FAMILY PASS
# xSample's own copy of the cache finds the player xTwin recorded: one board between them.
# `forget` asks to be typed again to confirm.
run: xsample forget FamilyProbe
run: xsample forget FamilyProbe
expect: Forgot FamilyProbe.
# xTwin forgets the player, which deletes them from the family's cache: one record.
run: xtwin forget FamilyProbe
run: xtwin forget FamilyProbe
expect: Records deleted: 1.
# And xSample no longer finds them.
run: xsample forget FamilyProbe
run: xsample forget FamilyProbe
expect: No player called FamilyProbe has played here.
