# Real clients on Fabric: mineflayer on 1.20.1 and 1.21.11, not 26.3 yet (2026-11-27)

Every rewritten plugin's Fabric jar was booted with console checks only: `scripts/boot-fabric.sh` couldn't drive a client, so messages to players, joins, menus, tab lists and chat on Fabric were only unit-tested (#162, Coverage). Now real clients join the Fabric boots on every pull request that touches Fabric.

- **The same step and scenarios as Bukkit.** `scripts/boot-fabric.sh` takes `client: [args]` steps (and `--client`) and `--seed` folders, as `scripts/boot-test.sh` does: it runs `scripts/budgets/bots/client.js` with the server's port, from the same `.cache/budget-bots/` packages, and fails unless the client ends with `CLIENT PASS`. `--seed scripts/boot-seeds/client` makes Joiner an operator through vanilla's `ops.json`, which Fabric reads as Paper does. The server is offline-mode, as the Bukkit boots are; nothing forwards from a proxy. Each rewritten plugin has a `fabric/*client-checks.txt` running its Bukkit scenario, with the plugins that scenario needs beside it (zChat for xMoney and xBans, xMoney for xTeleport and xMarket, zChat and xTeleport for xVanish) and Fabric's own seeds (`config/<mod>/config.yml`: `xmoney/fabric/zchat-seed`, `xteleport/fabric/xmoney-seed`).
- **`--fabric`, for what differs there by design.** `boot-fabric.sh` adds it to every client step. Two checks differ: xUtilities' `/back` is checked after a death, and refusing vanilla's `/tp`, because Fabric has no teleport event (the docs' platform table says so); xSample's `/xsample debug` answers in English, since the Fabric sandbox ships no `lang/` files. Every other check is the same as on Bukkit.
- **1.20.1 has no client language** (`FabricLanguages`; #162's Fabric section), so players there read the family's `default-language`. On 1.20.1 `boot-fabric.sh` also adds `--fixed-language`: wherever Joiner, in German, would read German, the client expects the English every player there reads, and the checks of a change of language are skipped. Setting `default-language: de` instead would have turned the console's replies German too, which the console checks read in English.
- **Where it runs:** the Fabric 1.20.1 cell, and a new per-PR Fabric 1.21.11 cell, both `client: true`, which adds Node.js, the bots' cache and each plugin's client checks. The 26.3 cell and Quilt stay console-only.
- **Why not 26.3: mineflayer 4.39.0** (its newest release, 2026-09) joins up to 26.1 (`testedVersions`), and its minecraft-data has no protocol for 26.2 or 26.3. Its 26.3 support is in open pull requests across four repositories (mineflayer #4124 and #4206, minecraft-data, node-minecraft-protocol, prismarine-chunk), so it can't be pinned yet. platform-fabric builds 1.20.1, 1.21.11 and 26.3, so 1.21.11 is the newest line a client joins. **Considered and not taken:** ViaFabric with ViaVersion and ViaBackwards on the 26.3 server, letting a 26.1 client in: ViaFabric's 26.3 builds are alpha, it's three more mods whose log lines the boot judges, and the translation sits between the plugin and the client, which is the thing under test. No other headless client is as cheap: a client on MCProtocolLib (which follows new versions quickly) would mean writing every scenario again in Java. When mineflayer publishes 26.3, bumping `scripts/budgets/bots/package.json` and giving the 26.3 cell `client: true` closes the gap (with Paper 26.3's client cell and the nightly load, decisions/2026-11-25-nightly-load-reports-startup.md).
- **What the clients found**, and fixed in their own pull requests:
  - xMarket on Fabric 1.21.11 and 26.3 refused to sell or price anything a player held (`/sell hand`, `/worth`, the sell menu): every item has a `minecraft:lore` component in its defaults, empty, so `stack.has(LORE)` was always true and no held item counted as plain. Lore now counts only with lines in it.
  - xVanish on Fabric listed a vanished player in the tab list of a player who joined after they vanished: Fabric's `JOIN` comes as Minecraft makes the connection, before it sends the joiner everyone's tab list entries, so the entry taken off then came back. It's taken off again on the next tick, both ways.
- **Passed locally** on Fabric 1.21.11 (Java 21) and 1.20.1 (Java 17), every scenario: xSample, xUtilities, zChat, xMoney with zChat, xTeleport with xMoney, xBans with zChat, xVanish with zChat and xTeleport, and xMarket with xMoney. A client adds 15 to 45 s to a plugin's boot (xBans' tempban waits 15 s).
