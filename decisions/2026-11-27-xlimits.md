# xLimits 1.0: limits checked as things happen, counted per chunk, on the core's world hooks (2026-11-27)

xLimits (M14, #111) is a new plugin: `xlimits/api`, `xlimits/common`, `xlimits/bukkit` and `xlimits/fabric`. What was decided:

- **The Bukkit floor is 1.13, as ROADMAP.md proposed.** Block data, entity scoreboard tags and the world hooks start there; Mob awareness (`no-ai`) is 1.15 and is left out below it with a warning, Paper's TPS and tick time and Folia's region TPS are found once at startup. Boot tests: Spigot 1.13.2 (Java 11), Paper 1.21.11 and 26.3, Folia 26.3, Fabric 1.20.1, 1.21.11 and 26.3, all green locally, and a real client on Paper 1.21.11 and Spigot 1.13.2, in German, has a spawn egg refused at a cow cap (the hooks name the player) and a second hopper refused, each with the message saying why; the 1.13.2 Spigot cell becomes a per-PR cell, the floor of xLimits (and xRewind, xStats, xPlots), and the boot step skips older cells.
- **Spawns, blocks, tames and deaths are the core's world hooks** (built with xRewind 2.0, ROADMAP.md: "shared block and entity hooks"), at HIGH, so protection plugins have refused first and a logger at MONITOR sees xLimits' refusals as refused. What only a limiter needs stays in xLimits' adapter, registered only while its limit is on: chunks loading (Paper's EntitiesLoadEvent from 1.17), redstone (BlockRedstoneEvent), TNT priming (Bukkit's TNTPrimeEvent from 1.19.4, Paper's own before, else the spawn refused and the block put back), falling blocks and portals.
- **No repeating tasks, nothing stored.** A chunk's entities are counted at most once a second, on the thread that owns it, and allowed spawns are added meanwhile, so a busy spawner costs one count a second. Its blocks are counted once (block entities from the chunk's list at once; other blocks from a snapshot off the server's threads, with what's placed meanwhile added after) and kept from placements and breaks. Entries are forgotten as chunks unload. Load profiles are checked as limits are, at most once a second; on Folia each chunk's entry keeps its region's profile and TPS reading, so no region tracking is needed.
- **Farm slowing marks the mob, not a file.** Scoreboard tags (`xlimits.slowed`, and one per action changed) say what to undo, so a restart or a crash never strands a mob, and a chunk that loads with marked mobs under its threshold is restored. Restoring waits until the count is 10% under the threshold, so a farm doesn't flip. On Fabric, collisions go through a scoreboard team that never collides, since Minecraft has no per-mob switch.
- **Load profiles' hysteresis.** The strictest profile whose `on` holds turns on at once; the one on turns off only past its `off` and after `minimum-time`, then the strictest one below it that hasn't reached its own `off` takes over. A profile set the wrong way round turns off at its `on`.
- **Cancelling `LimitReachedEvent` allows the thing**, as #111 asked: a plugin overrides one refusal; a `LimitProvider` changes limits for an area (plots, claims, arenas) and is the xPlots integration's seam.
- **Importers write config.yml**, through the core's config editor, keeping the owner's values: FarmControl's proactive profiles (count as threshold; `kill` as a cap one under it, since xLimits refuses rather than kills; reactive mode as an `mspt-above` profile) and Insights' `GROUP` limits. Both counted per chunk where the other plugin counted within a distance or region; the docs say to check the numbers.
- **Jars over 500 KiB, recorded:** 632 KiB on Bukkit, 539 to 547 KiB on Fabric. About 400 KiB is the shared core with its world hooks, about 200 KiB xLimits' own and its API, the rest the startup list and the translations. On Paper 26.3 (M1 Pro, load average 80 from other boots, so loose): 44.3 ms startup with the startup list (median of 5), 1.352 MB metaspace in its own loaders, 2.7 MB heap over the server without it (21 KB of its own objects; the rest moves with the base server), idle 0. CI's budgets job is the measure.
- **The startup list leaves out the core's `PaperLibraryLoader`.** It's Java 17 class files, which Spigot 1.13 to 1.17 can't read as they rewrite a plugin's classes, so preloading it logged an error there (xVanish's list has the same line); Paper loads it itself first anyway.
- **Not in 1.0:** bStats, which has no service id for xLimits yet (none is sent); on Fabric, redstone, TNT, falling blocks and portals (no events), `no-ai` and `no-breeding`, TPS and MSPT profiles, and pets already tamed before a start; on Folia, `/xlimits top` and `restore` reach the chunks around online players only (Folia has no list of every loaded chunk), with chunks restored as they load until a reload; spark profiles at the limits, which come with the alpha on our own servers. The brand catalog entry (glyph, headline) is the brand repo's, before the release; until then the docs give xLimits its name (versions.json).
