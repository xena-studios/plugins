# xPlots 2.0: 1.x's plots on Paper, Folia and Spigot from 1.13, a region seam for later platforms, and renewals charged once (2026-11-28)

M13 (#132): xPlots rewritten in place on the rewrite's core, as `xplots/api`, `common` and `bukkit`, on Paper, Folia, Spigot and CraftBukkit from 1.13 (block data's first version). Fabric and NeoForge come later, with xPlots' own regions in WorldGuard's place. Laid out, migrated, documented and boot-tested as xRewind 2.0 was ([xRewind 2.0](2026-11-27-xrewind-2.md)); the Java 1.x plugin is gone from the tree (the `maintenance` branch keeps 1.x). What was decided:

- **1.x's tables, byte for byte.** `field`, `plot`, `lease`, `member` and `snapshot` keep 1.x's columns, types and schema version (1), made with 1.x's statements; a field's own settings are 1.x's `price=128;reset=false` text, signs' facings `BlockFace` names, and snapshots 1.x's bytes exactly (a version byte, sizes, `writeUTF` palette, varint indices, deflated with a zlib header: `MigrationTest.snapshotsAreByteForByte1xs`). 2.0's one addition, `renewal`, is a table made beside the schema (`CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` after migrating), not a step of it, so the schema stays 1 and the 1.x jar opens the database and ignores the table; 2.0's new field setting, `auto-renew`, is one 1.x's settings parser already skips. Going back is putting the old jar back and its config aside: on the upgrade page, tested against a 1.x database.
- **1.x's config paths are 2.0's.** Config version 2; `migrate(1)` moves every text to `lang/en.yml` (an owner's edits carried, `messages.<x>` becoming `<x>`, the signs' lists and menu lore joined with `<newline>`, `menus.<m>.<b>.name`/`lore` and `.title` keeping their paths), the theme into `messages.theme`, `update-checker` into `updates`. `plots`, `currency` (1.x's `vault` kept: it means the economy, 2.0's `economy`), `max-plots`, the signs' material and placement, the menus' materials, `teleport-warmup`, `reset-ms-per-tick` and `storage` stay. Staff replies, English-only code in 1.x, are messages now (`staff.*`).
- **Protection's decisions are common's; the listeners are each platform's.** `Protection` answers "may this player change this block" and "does this change cross a plot's edge" from memory, on any thread (a Folia region's): the grid's arithmetic, signs by block key, an owner index, each world's index replaced whole as it changes. Bukkit's listeners are 1.x's events, version-checked (a lectern's book and TNT priming are classes only 1.14 and 1.19.4 servers load). Until the database is read, which 2.0 does off the server thread, every change a player makes is refused and every change that could cross into a field stopped: 1.x read synchronously so a plot was never unprotected, and this keeps that.
- **The region seam is the API's `RegionBackend`.** WorldGuard on Bukkit is one backend (1.x's one region per field, plus one per plot made outside its field, allowing build/use/chest/interact at priority 1, so a world-wide `build deny` doesn't block plots); another plugin registers its own, told about every field there is at once and every one made or deleted after. Fabric's and NeoForge's own regions will be a backend and that platform's listeners on the same `Protection`. Not chosen: a region model of xPlots' own in common now: 1.x protects by its grid, not by regions, and a backend only needs to know where fields are.
- **WorldGuard and WorldEdit by name.** Their newest jars are Java 21 class files whose vectors are records, which xPlots' Java 8 classes can't compile against, so `EngineHub.kt` calls them through reflection, looked up once through their own class loader; the calls are the same in every version from 7.0.0 (1.13's) to the newest. Boot-tested with WorldGuard 7.0.0 and WorldEdit 7.0.0 on Spigot 1.13.2, and WorldGuard 7.0.15/7.0.21 with WorldEdit 7.4.6 on Paper 1.21.11 and 26.3, fetched from Modrinth and checked against their sha256 (`boot.sh --worldguard`). **Gaps:** WorldGuard doesn't run on Folia (EngineHub doesn't support it), so there xPlots protects alone, as it always does inside fields; FastAsyncWorldEdit is accepted for `/xplots select` but not boot-tested; on Spigot WorldEdit warns at startup that it works better on Paper, which the boot allows.
- **Renewals are charged once, even across a crash.** `plots.auto-renew` (default off, 1.x's behaviour) renews a plot an hour before it runs out, from the owner's balance, online or not: the charge is written to `renewal.charge` as the expiry it extends from, and only once that write commits is the money taken, with `#<expiry>` in the ledger's reason; the new expiry and the cleared charge are one write (one transaction). A start that finds a charge left asks the economy's ledger (xMoney's `history`) whether that payment was made: found, or no ledger to ask (Vault), it's taken as paid and the lease extended without charging; not found, the charge is dropped and tried again in its time. A balance that doesn't cover it is tried once per expiry; the owner is told (on join if away). Tested with a payment taken and the server stopped before xPlots heard it went through (`aRenewalCutShortByARestartIsNeverChargedTwice`).
- **The family by API, each through its own class loader.** Each Xena plugin relocates its own core, so another's API takes its core's types: xMoney's (`XMoneyBridge`: has, withdraw, deposit, format, history), xTeleport's extra-homes seam (`registerHomes` with a `java.lang.reflect.Proxy` `HomeProvider`, each plot home made as xTeleport's `Home`), xRewind's rollback within a region (`Query.Builder.area` with the player and time, planned and applied as `/xrewind rollback` is), and xVanish's `canSee` for the member picker and `/plots add`'s completion, each by name. Without one, its feature is off. Rent goes through xMoney when it's there, Vault's economy otherwise; item currency stays 1.x's, and can't renew (an item isn't in a balance).
- **Menus on the core's chest menus** ([menus in the core](2026-11-27-menus-in-the-core.md)): 1.x's layouts and buttons, each drawn in the viewer's language; heads by the platform's own item data. Signs are in the server's language, being one text for everyone.
- **Threads.** Changes to plots are made under one lock from whichever thread makes them (a command, a menu click, a payment the economy confirmed later, the global timer); reads need none. Signs are written on their place's thread, one task per chunk for the minute's refresh; a reset or a snapshot is a job a few milliseconds a tick on the plot's own thread, one job at a time, its chunks loaded in the background and held with plugin tickets (Paper 1.14 on) until a few seconds after the last job, so they don't unload before the capture runs.
- **The API, xPlots' first:** `XPlots.api()`: fields and plots from memory as `PlotInfo` copies, `plotAt`, `mayBuild`, `setOwner`, `evict`, `addMember`, `removeMember`, `extend` and `price`; cancellable pre-events and post-events for claiming, ending, members and rent (renewals included), each a Bukkit event too (`Bukkit*Event`, async when fired off the server thread); price modifiers, access rules and region backends as add-ons. ABI dumped in `xplots/api/api/`.
- **Startup and memory.** On Paper 26.3 (an M1 Pro other agents' boots were loading to 45; CI's two-core budgets job is the final word): metaspace 1.468 MB of the 1.5 MB with the recorded startup list, heap +2.8 MB, idle 0 on the server thread and off it, startup 78-123 ms with the machine quieter. The first build was at 1.593 MB without a list: what brought it down, each the lighter core's method ([a lighter core](2026-11-25-a-lighter-core.md)): renting, renewals, signs, the timer, the API's add-ons, selections and confirmations each made with its first use; the timers run only once a field exists; the store's writer made with the first change, apart from its one-time reader; Bukkit's blocks, signs, items and region hooks (`BukkitWorld`) apart from the players' side; the menus' eight config sections one class.
- **Size: the jar is over the 500 KiB target, and stays so.** Shrunk: 863 KiB (Bukkit, with its startup list). About 420 KiB is the shared core xPlots reaches (as xRewind's); the rest xPlots' own with its API: the grid, the index and protection's decisions, 1.x's listeners, the store, renting and renewals with their crash recovery, signs, snapshots and resets, the menus, staff's commands, the config migration, four bridges to the family and two to EngineHub, the ten Bukkit events, and seven translations. 1.x's 280 KiB ran on Paper 1.21.11+ alone, on the old core, with no API. Cutting it would cut features 1.x has or the integrations the milestone asks for.
- **Boot matrix:** xPlots boots on every Bukkit cell from 1.13 (older ones skip it), with WorldGuard and WorldEdit on Paper and Spigot 1.13.2; beside xMoney, xTeleport, xRewind and xVanish on `family` and `client` cells and at its floor; in the family test from 1.13; and real clients on Paper 1.21.11 and Spigot 1.13.2 (German: a field made, a plot rented through its sign and menu paid through xMoney, a non-member refused, a member let build, the plot home through xTeleport's `/home`). `scripts/affected.sh` no longer boots Fabric for a plugin with no `fabric/`.
- **Not done:** native review of the seven drafts; Fabric and NeoForge (with their regions); a region backend for GriefPrevention or the like (the seam is there); an xLimits limit provider per plot (its docs say xPlots 2.0 will register one; not in this milestone's list); offline owners told about a renewal only within the same server run (the notice isn't stored).
