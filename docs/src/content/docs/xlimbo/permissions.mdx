`/join` is everyone's: `xlimbo.join` by default, or whatever `join-command.permission` names. `xlimbo.admin` grants the admin subcommands (`reload`, `debug`, `forget`) and the update notices, as 2.x's `xlimbo.admin` granted `reload` and `info`; each is also on its own as `xlimbo.admin.<subcommand>`. 3.0's staff commands have their own, operators' by default: `xlimbo.list`, `xlimbo.send` and `xlimbo.back`.
