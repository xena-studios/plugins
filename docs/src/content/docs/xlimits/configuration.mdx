`plugins/xLimits/config.yml` (`config/xlimits/` on Fabric). Everything applies on `/xlimits reload`. A feature that's off registers no listener.

## Names

A limit's name is an entity group, an entity type, a block, or a group of your own:

- **Entity groups:** `animals`, `monsters`, `villagers`, `water` (fish, squid, axolotls, dolphins), `ambient` (bats), `golems`, `vehicles` (boats and minecarts), `armor_stands`, `item_frames` (with paintings).
- **Entity types and blocks** by their Minecraft id: `cow`, `zombified_piglin`, `hopper`, `sticky_piston`.
- **Your own groups:** `entities.groups` and `blocks.groups`, a name and a list each, counted together.

Every limit that matches is checked: with `animals: 80` and `cow: 20`, the 21st cow is refused even with room for more animals.

**Per world:** `worlds.overrides` sets any limit differently in one world; `worlds.exempt` leaves a world alone. **Per area:** other plugins (a plot plugin, an arena plugin) change limits or exempt an area through the [API](/xlimits/developer-api/).

## Load profiles

```yaml
profiles:
  enabled: true
  minimum-time: 2m
  list:
    busy:
      when: players-online-above
      on: 80
      off: 70
      scale: 0.8
    strained:
      when: tps-below
      on: 17
      off: 19
      scale: 0.5
      limits:
        hopper: 40
```

A profile turns on when its condition passes `on`, and turns off once it's back past `off` and has been on for `minimum-time`, so limits don't flip back and forth. A later profile wins over an earlier one; when the later one turns off, the earlier one takes over if its own condition still holds. `scale` multiplies every limit and farm threshold; `limits` set some outright. A profile only tightens new spawns, placements and the farm thresholds: nothing in the world is removed. Staff are told at each switch, and `/xlimits here` shows which is on.

`players-online-above` works everywhere; `tps-below` on Paper (the server's TPS) and Folia (the region's, from `getRegionTPS`); `mspt-above` on Paper only, from its average tick time, which shows trouble at 40 to 45 ms while TPS still says 20. On Folia each region has its own profile: a farm that makes one region lag tightens only that region.
