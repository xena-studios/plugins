Operators have every permission but `xlimits.bypass`, which nobody has until it's given: with it, a player places blocks, uses spawn eggs, places vehicles, breeds and tames past every limit. Natural spawns, spawners, redstone, TNT and portals are never bypassed.

`xlimits.alerts` gets the staff alerts.
