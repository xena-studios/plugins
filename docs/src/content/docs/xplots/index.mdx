Current version: **2.0.0**

xPlots rents small plots to players for in-game money, the box-server staple. Instead of a region and a sign for every plot made by hand, you select the whole area, say how big each plot is, and confirm: every plot and every sign is made for you.

- **Every feature free.** No premium version, no early-access builds, nothing behind a Patreon.
- **On every server you run.** One jar for Paper, Folia, Spigot and CraftBukkit from 1.13 to the newest. WorldGuard and WorldEdit are used when they're there, never needed.
- **Setup in a minute.** One selection becomes a grid of plots with paths between them, outlined in particles before anything changes. Signs go up on their own. See [Setting up](/xplots/setting-up/).
- **Plots come back clean.** Each plot is saved when it's made and put back when its rent runs out, a few milliseconds per tick.
- **Rent that renews itself.** With `auto-renew`, a plot renews from its owner's balance an hour before it runs out, online or not, charged once even across a crash.
- **You won't feel it running.** Finding a block's plot is arithmetic on the grid, the same for ten plots or ten thousand. One timer for the next lease to end, and a once-a-minute check of signs in loaded chunks; with no field, nothing runs at all.
- **With the family.** Rent through [xMoney](/xmoney/) (each payment in its ledger), plot homes in [xTeleport](/xteleport/)'s `/homes`, an owner's `/plots rollback` through [xRewind](/xrewind/), and [xVanish](/xvanish/)'s hidden players left out of the member picker.
- **In your players' languages.** Every message, sign and menu in each player's client's language: English, German, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish or Simplified Chinese.

:::note[Coming from xPlots 1.x?]
Replace the jar and restart: your fields, plots, rents and saved blocks are read as they are, your config is carried forward, your edited messages, sign lines and menu texts move to `lang/en.yml`, and the old file is kept as `config.yml.bak-v1`. See [Upgrading from 1.x](/xplots/upgrade/).
:::

## Where it runs

<Reference plugin="xplots" show="platforms" />

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Paper** | Everything. Chunks load off the server thread to save and put back plots, held with a ticket while they're worked on; teleports don't block. |
| **Folia** | Everything, each sign and each plot's reset on the thread of its own region. WorldGuard doesn't run on Folia, so xPlots protects its fields alone there, as it always does: a field's region is only for a world-wide `build deny`. |
| **Spigot and CraftBukkit** | Everything, from 1.13 (block data's first version). Chunks load on the server thread. Signs can't be edited once placed before 1.20, so there's no wax to add. |
| **Fabric and NeoForge** | Later, with xPlots' own regions in place of WorldGuard's (the plugin's logic is already the same on every platform). |

## How it works

1. Staff select an area with `/xplots wand` (or WorldEdit) and run `/xplots field create box size:5 path:2`.
2. Players click a free plot's sign, check the price in a menu, and rent it.
3. `/plots` manages it: pay ahead, members, member slots, renewing, a teleport home, or giving it up.
4. When the rent runs out, the plot is put back as it was made and its sign says it's free again.

## For developers

Read every field and plot, rent them out and change members behind cancellable events, change prices, decide who may build, or tell another region system about every field: the [Developer API](/xplots/developer-api/).
