`<angle brackets>` are required, `[square brackets]` optional. Aliases are the defaults; change them in the [config](/xteleport/configuration/), where every command can also be turned off or given another permission. Player commands are everyone's by default, `/setwarp`, `/delwarp` and `/setspawn` operators'.

- **Staff and other players' homes:** with `permission-others` (`xteleport.homes.others`), `/home <player>:<home>`, `/delhome <player>:<home>` and `/homes <player>`, for players who are offline too, as long as they've played here.
- **`/tpaccept`** and **`/tpdeny`** answer the newest request, or the named player's. **`/tpacancel`** takes back every request you sent, or the one to that player.
- **`/rtp [world]`** goes to `rtp.default-world`, or the world you're in, without one. A short cooldown (`rtp.search-cooldown`) starts with each search, found or not, and the command's own when you arrive.
- **`/xteleport import essentials`** is a dry run until `confirm`: see [Switching](/xteleport/switching/).
- **`/xteleport rtpcheck <world> [searches]`** runs `/rtp`'s search, teleporting no one, and says how often it found a spot: for trying a world's settings.
- **`/xteleport forget <player>`** deletes a player's homes and TPA setting.

## Placeholders

Answered from memory. With [PlaceholderAPI](https://modrinth.com/plugin/placeholderapi) on Bukkit they're `%xteleport_homes%` and so on; `cooldown_` takes any command's name, or `rtp-search` for `/rtp`'s search cooldown.
