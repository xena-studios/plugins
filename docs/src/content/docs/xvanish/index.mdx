Current version: **1.0.0**

xVanish hides staff from players everywhere a player could notice them, keeps them hidden through relogs and restarts, and runs on Folia.

- **Every feature free.** No premium version: silent containers and network sync included.
- **Hidden everywhere.** The world and the tab list (Bukkit's own `hidePlayer`, so every other plugin's `canSee` is false), join and quit messages (with fake ones as you vanish and show again), the server list's count and sample, the locator bar, death and advancement messages, sleeping and mob spawning.
- **No trace.** Mobs don't target a vanished player, nothing is picked up (unless `/vanish pickup`), no pressure plates, tripwires, farmland or turtle eggs, no sculk sensors, no collisions, projectiles pass through, no raids. Each is a switch in the config.
- **Silent containers.** A chest, barrel or shulker box opens as a read-only copy, with no lid moving and no sound. Sneak to open it as usual.
- **Levels.** `xvanish.see` sees vanished staff; `xvanish.level.<n>` hides senior staff from junior staff.
- **The family knows.** zChat, xTeleport and the other Xena plugins leave a vanished player out of mentions, `/msg`, `/tpa` and their lists. See [Integrations](/xvanish/integrations/).
- **In your players' languages.** Every message reaches each player in their client's language.
- **Easy to switch to.** `/sv` and `/pv` keep SuperVanish's and PremiumVanish's habits, their permissions can be honoured as they are, and who was vanished is imported with one command. See [Switching](/xvanish/switching/).

## Where it runs

One jar for every Bukkit-family server from 1.8.8 to the newest, Folia included, and a jar per Minecraft line on Fabric, server-side only: players install nothing.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Paper and Folia** | Everything. On Folia a player vanishing is hidden from everyone at once on their own thread (`setVisibleByDefault`), then shown to the staff who may see them, each on their own thread, so nobody sees them for a tick. |
| **Spigot and CraftBukkit** | Everything but advancement messages and mob spawning, which only Paper lets a plugin change. Some switches need a newer Minecraft: collisions and the boss bar 1.9, raids 1.14, sculk 1.17, the locator bar 1.21.6; projectiles pass through from Spigot 1.20.4, and before that only do no harm. |
| **Fabric** | Hidden from other players' tab list and world, join, quit, death and advancement messages left out, invulnerable, chat held back, flight, night vision, the boss bar and the locator bar. Fabric has no events for the rest (the server list, targeting, pickup, physical blocks, sculk, raids, silent containers, collisions, sleeping and spawning), so those switches do nothing there. Showing a player again re-sends their own chunks once. |
| **Placeholders** | `%xvanish_vanished%` and the rest with [PlaceholderAPI](https://modrinth.com/plugin/placeholderapi) on Bukkit. |

## How it works

**`/vanish`** (or `/v`, `/sv`, `/pv`) vanishes you, and again shows you. Staff with `xvanish.vanish.others` vanish others: `/vanish <player>`, `/vanish on <player>`, `/vanish off <player>`. `/vanish list` shows who's vanished.

**Staying vanished.** Who's vanished is kept in `plugins/xVanish/vanished.txt`, written as it changes, so a relog, a restart or a crash never shows anyone. `xvanish.join` vanishes a player every time they join, silently.

**Seeing the vanished.** Staff with `xvanish.see` see vanished players whose level is at most their own; a player's level is the highest `xvanish.level.<n>` they have, 1 without one. With xBans, the staff it counts (`xbans.staff`) see level 1 too.

**Light.** No repeating tasks: state changes on a command, a join or a quit, and the boss bar stays on screen by itself. A switch that's off registers no listener, and each listener first asks whether anyone is vanished at all, from memory. No database on a single server, and no packets or NMS on Bukkit.

**Networks.** Turn on `network.enabled` and point `storage` at a MySQL or MariaDB database every server shares: a vanished player stays vanished as they move between servers, and `/vanish list` covers the network. See [Configuration](/xvanish/configuration/).

## Admin commands

`/xvanish reload`, `/xvanish debug`, `/xvanish forget <player>` and `/xvanish import supervanish|premiumvanish`. See [Commands](/xvanish/commands/).
