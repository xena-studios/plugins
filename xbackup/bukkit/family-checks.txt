# The family (boot.sh --family): xMoney 2.0 and xBans 2.0 beside xBackup, on SQLite. Each
# backup asks their own database writers for a copy (core's family backup seam), never copies
# their files from outside; the status says so, and the copies check and restore.
logged: xMoney
logged: xBans

run: backup now family
expect: Backed up
run: backup status
expect: Databases copied by their plugin: xBans, xMoney
run: backup verify full
expect: All good.
