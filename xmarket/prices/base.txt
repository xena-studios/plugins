# xMarket's base prices: what one of each item costs to buy, for the items nothing is made from
# (and a few that are, where the recipe isn't how players get them). Every other item's price
# is worked out from its recipes (Pricing), so crafting can never be cheaper than buying.
# What an item sells for is a share of what it costs (sell-percent, 30 by default).
#
# How they were chosen: by how much time and risk one takes to get in survival, on one scale.
#   1      a block anyone digs by the stack: dirt, cobblestone, sand, netherrack
#   2-5    a common plant, crop or block that takes a little looking: logs (4), wheat, flowers
#   5-20   a common mob drop or mineral: bone, string, coal (5), copper, redstone, iron (20)
#   20-60  a drop that takes a fight or a trip: ender pearls, blaze rods, gold (40), slime
#   100+   rare finds: emeralds (100), diamonds (200), music discs, sherds, armour trims
#   1000+  boss, structure and once-a-world loot: ancient debris, tridents, totems, the dragon
# Raw ores (Silk Touch) cost a little more than what they smelt to.
