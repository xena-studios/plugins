xMoney bundles VaultUnlockedAPI

To stand in for Vault, this jar includes the classes of VaultUnlockedAPI 2.20,
unmodified, in the packages net.milkbowl.vault (Vault's API) and
net.milkbowl.vault2 (VaultUnlocked's API):

  published by the Free Software Foundation, either version 3 of the License, or
  (at your option) any later version."

Those classes are under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3
(LGPL-3.0.txt here), which builds on the GNU General Public License, version 3
(GPL-3.0.txt here). They are kept unrelocated and unmodified, so they can be
replaced with another version of the same library: swap the classes under
net/milkbowl/ in this jar, or rebuild xMoney from its source with another
version. Their source is at the links above.
