# its API, by name through its own class loader. Rent goes through xMoney's ledger; a rollback in
# a plot through xRewind's. A real client checks renting, members and plot homes
# (client-checks.txt).
run: xplots info
expect: Rent: xMoney.
run: xplots select world 400 64 400 411 70 404
expect: Selected

expect: = 2 plots of
