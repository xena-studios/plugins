**Vanish for staff, on Paper, Folia, Spigot and Fabric.**

xVanish hides staff from players everywhere a player could notice them, keeps them hidden through relogs and restarts, and never runs a task it doesn't need.

- **Every feature free.** Silent containers and network sync included: no premium version.
- **Hidden everywhere.** The world and the tab list (Bukkit's own `hidePlayer`, so every plugin's `canSee` agrees), join and quit messages with fake ones, the server list, the locator bar, death and advancement messages, sleeping and mob spawning.
- **No trace in the world.** No targeting, pickup, pressure plates, farmland, sculk, collisions, raids; projectiles pass through. Each one a switch.
- **Silent containers.** Look inside chests, barrels and shulker boxes without a lid moving or a sound.
- **Levels.** `xvanish.see` and `xvanish.level.<n>` keep senior staff hidden from junior staff.
- **On Folia**, with every player hidden on their own thread, and nobody seeing a vanished player for even a tick.
- **Works with the family.** zChat's mentions and `/msg`, xTeleport's `/tpa` and every Xena plugin leave vanished staff out.
- **Easy to switch to.** `/sv` and `/pv`, SuperVanish's and PremiumVanish's permissions, and who was vanished imported with one command.
- **In your players' languages**: English, German, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish and Simplified Chinese.

**[Docs](https://plugins.xenastudios.co/xvanish/)** · [Commands](https://plugins.xenastudios.co/xvanish/commands/) · [Switching from SuperVanish](https://plugins.xenastudios.co/xvanish/switching/) · [Developer API](https://plugins.xenastudios.co/xvanish/developer-api/)

Runs on Paper, Folia, Spigot and CraftBukkit from 1.8.8, and Fabric from 1.20.1 (server-side only). PlaceholderAPI is optional.
